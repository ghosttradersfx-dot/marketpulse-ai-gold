Methodology & limitations

TradingView's embedded chart and XAUS reference-data analysis feed are separate. Their prices and candle times can differ. The analysis API uses XAU/USD OHLC candles and calculates EMA20, EMA50, RSI14, MACD(12,26,9), ATR14, and recent 20-candle high/low. The latest returned candle may be unfinished. This is educational research, not an automated trading system. No orders are placed. The app does not fabricate data when the provider is unavailable.
